Mandaadi

**Mandaadi** is a Tamil romantic drama movie about love, family, and sacrifice. The story follows Mandaadi, a loving husband who raises his children on his own. He is curious about his wife and wants to know what happened to her. When Mandaadi meets with an accident and falls into a coma, his children begin to tell him the story of their past. They explain how they met their mother, how they fell in love, got married, and started their family. Their happy life changes when Mandaadi's wife is diagnosed with stage-three blood cancer while she is pregnant. Mandaadi stays by her side and supports her through the difficult treatment. Despite facing many struggles, their love for each other remains strong.
