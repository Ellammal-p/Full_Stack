Igloo

**Igloo** is a Tamil romantic drama movie about love, family, and sacrifice. The story follows Shiva, a loving father who raises his twin daughters, Vaishu and Aishu, on his own. Vaishu is curious about her mother and wants to know what happened to her. When Vaishu meets with an accident and falls into a coma, Shiva begins to tell her the story of his past. He explains how he met Ramya, how they fell in love, got married, and started their family. Their happy life changes when Ramya is diagnosed with stage-three blood cancer while she is pregnant. Shiva stays by her side and supports her through the difficult treatment. Despite facing many struggles, their love for each other remains strong. The movie shows the importance of **love, family, sacrifice, hope, and a parent's unconditional love for their children**.
